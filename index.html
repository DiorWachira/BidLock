              HOW A SHOW RUNS

              1. DraftSix rounds (eight in Marathon), one category each. One lot per contender each round, everyone takes exactly one, and the weakest lot always comes last.
              2. CratesTwo sealed mystery crates arrive between rounds. Jackpot or curse, sold blind. The photograph appears when it is opened.
              3. OracleFrom round three, the Oracle may pick one contender for a hard question. Right: steal any lot at half price, or gamble on a second question to take it free.
              4. VaultTen seconds. Pay $2 to lock a lot so it cannot be stolen.
              5. HeistTen seconds. One steal each: pay what the owner paid plus $1 and swap lots.
              6. VerdictSealed values are revealed. Three matching tags pay +40. Highest total wins.

              GLOSSARY

              Lot
              One thing being sold. It opens at $1.
              Shot clock
              Five seconds. Every bid resets it. At zero the hammer falls.
              Hammer price
              What the winner paid. It also sets what a thief must pay later.
              Max bid
              Your cash minus $1 for every other slot you still have to fill.
              All-In
              Play without that $1 reserve. Empty slots become Penalty Duds.
              Penalty Dud
              A $0 filler worth nothing, handed to anyone who ends a round empty-handed.
              Hype
              What the room thinks a lot is worth. Public, and not always right.
              True value
              The sealed score, 1 to 100, shown only at the verdict.
              Synergy
              Three lots sharing a tag pay +40. Five pay +80.
              Mystery crate
              A blind lot outside the rounds. It adds to your total if it is good.
              Oracle
              A general-knowledge question for one contender. A right answer buys a steal at half the owner's price, and the owner gets that half back.
              Double or nothing
              After a right answer: take the half-price steal, or risk it on a second question. Right is free. Wrong loses the steal.
              Vault
              $2 to make one lot heist-proof.
              Heist
              A forced swap. The thief pays hammer price plus $1 and hands over their own lot from that category.
              Uncontested
              The last contender still needing a lot gets the last one, the round's booby prize, for $1.
              Room code
              Five characters, like BL-7K9. The host's browser runs the match; everyone else connects straight to it.

              THE HOUSE RIVALS
